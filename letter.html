Title: home/shelter

You were always my home.
My safe place whenever I no longer felt like myself.
You didn't just shelter me when I needed comfort—you nourished me with love, patience, and affection.
What I felt for you was never temporary.
My heart never imagined you as a passing chapter.
Every promise I made carried the weight of a future—a life where it was always you and me, not simply falling in love, but choosing each other every day.
Building together. Growing together. Staying together.
In you, I found everything I had ever searched for.
I saw a person I could laugh with, dream with, grow with, and one day build a home beside. So if you ever wonder whether you were home or merely shelter until I found one...
Know this:
You were never just shelter.
You have always been home shorty.

Love Always ❤️

— Isthandwa Sakho, njengoba uyisithandwa sam ❤️
